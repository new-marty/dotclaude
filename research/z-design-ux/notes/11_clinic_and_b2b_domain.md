## 1. What do existing Japanese clinic booking products cover (features, screens, booking models)?

### Takeaway
Products converge on: three booking models (順番/時間/時間帯), web+LINE patient booking, Web問診, reminders, in-clinic queue display/kiosk, EMR/レセコン integration, and admin-side 診察枠/shift/stop-limit settings. Screen-level manuals were not retrievable; only vendor feature descriptions and selection articles (all vendor-authored) were found. No real case study with UX evidence exists in what I found.

### Cited Findings
- Three booking types: 順番予約 (queue; place in line, time not fixed), 時間予約 (fixed time), 時間帯予約 (broad band, combines both; less time deviation than queue, more slack than fixed time). Recommended by specialty: internal medicine/pediatrics (urgent walk-ins mix) = 順番 or 時間帯; exams and 自由診療 = 時間予約; dermatology/orthopedics = 時間帯 to level crowding; mixed clinics need a product running 順番 and 時間 side by side. (vendor) — [Medicom Park column](https://www.phchd.com/jp/medicom/park/idea/management-reservationsystem), [一創 column](https://www.issoh.co.jp/column/details/15271/), [MEDISMA](https://medisma.jp/column/reserve010/)
- A selection article says to check on the 受付 screen whether a same-day walk-in can be inserted into the reservation line easily and whether later appointments can be shifted en bloc after an emergency (search-tool summary of aggregator listing, not opened) — [search result set](https://www.issoh.co.jp/column/details/15271/) (weak; treat as hypothesis)
- Product catalogue by type (24 products, m3-run media with a self-promoted lead product, vendor-affiliated): 総合型 (メディカル革命, ヨヤクル, アポクル予約, Medical.M, リザーブリンク for multi-site, Dr.JOY予約 for referral booking), LINE特化型 (3Bees, CureSmile), 順番待ち特化型 (順番待ちの見える化くん, Wakumy, メディシェ), ポータル連携型 (EPARKクリニック・病院, ドクターズ・ファイル アポ), 電話自動応答型 (Call-Reserve, Reservia), 汎用型 (RESERVA, STORES予約, AirRESERVE). CLINICS, メディカルフォース, 予約ラボ, Apotool, デンタルアイ were NOT in this list; dentistry not covered. — [m3 digikar](https://digikar.m3.com/articles/system/article69)
- Specialty-specific needs: pediatrics = sibling simultaneous booking + auto reminders; derm/ENT/eye = queue management + in-clinic display; 自由診療 = cashless payment and firm advance booking. (vendor) — [m3 digikar](https://digikar.m3.com/articles/system/article69)
- EPARK function list (vendor): admin screen to check reception status and set closed days; 診療可能時間 input updates availability automatically; 時間枠受付; メニュー枠管理 (hours per menu); 院内ディスプレイ (waiting count); 再来受付機 (barcode card or IC); EMR/レセコン link; 簡易CTI (voice guidance before phone answered); patient side: online 順番受付, reminder email, push call-up when turn approaches ("スマホde呼び出し"), digital 診察券 app (history, auto check-in, requires download), digital 問診票. Not described there: multi-department, permissions, visit status. — [FDoc/EPARK function page](https://fdoc.jp/docs/for_clinic/lp/reservation/function.html)
- Slot design rules (vendor MEDISMA): split slot length by 初診 (e.g., 30 min) vs 再診 (e.g., 10 min); per-doctor availability (e.g., mornings only); slots per menu (vaccination only on certain weekdays); do not bunch 初診 slots (delays cascade); do not over-split menus (patients hesitate, inquiries rise); decide exception rules in advance (where to insert emergencies, how many minutes late is tolerated, how to re-release same-day cancellation slots); need a UI that registers phone bookings instantly; roles: reception changes bookings/adjusts slots, 院長 owns clinic-slot/closure settings. — [MEDISMA column](https://medisma.jp/column/reserve010/)
- Cancellation and reminders (vendor): put cancel/change link in reminders; reminders day-before and/or morning-of; one-tap cancel (claim: phone-only cancel raises psychological barrier and leads to no-shows); show cancel policy on booking screen; auto re-release freed slots; prepayment/deposit for 自由診療; channels email/SMS/LINE (SMS high reach but pay-per-send; LINE open rate claimed high); queue systems send "N people ahead" notice. Another vendor says one reminder the day before works best. No cancellation deadline example given. — [MEDISMA](https://medisma.jp/column/reserve010/), [一創](https://www.issoh.co.jp/column/details/15271/)
- Patient entry/ID: web and LINE booking identified by 診察券番号 or phone number, 24h; separate website entry points for 初診, same-day 順番受付, 再診 are recommended. (vendor) — [一創](https://www.issoh.co.jp/column/details/15271/)
- Web問診: required vs optional fields per department; too many fields cause drop-off. (vendor) — [MEDISMA](https://medisma.jp/column/reserve010/)
- Older patients (vendor): keep phone, assign roles (phone = exceptions/consultation; web/LINE = routine bookings); support proxy booking and next-visit booking at the window; large type, fewer steps, plain wording; introduce LINE notifications first as a stepping stone. — [MEDISMA](https://medisma.jp/column/reserve010/), [一創](https://www.issoh.co.jp/column/details/15271/)
- Purchase sequence (vendor): choose EMR/レセコン first because its integration spec constrains the booking system; check one-way vs two-way integration and whether chart receives booking/Web問診 data; check booking caps, SMS fees, separate integration charges; test 予約の受付停止・上限設定 for sudden closures in a demo; online-visit identity verification if relevant. — [一創](https://www.issoh.co.jp/column/details/15271/), [MEDISMA](https://medisma.jp/column/reserve010/)
- Expansion checks (vendor): multi-branch, external integrations (EMR, billing, CRM), CSV export, price changes, data handling on cancellation. — [MEDISMA](https://medisma.jp/column/reserve010/)
- Wait-time causes: crowding at opening, around lunch, near closing; reservation systems spread arrivals but do not fix root cause; slot design and same-day flow both need designing. (vendor consultant column, 2022) — [Medicom Park](https://www.phchd.com/jp/medicom/park/idea/management-reservationsystem), [MEDISMA](https://medisma.jp/column/reserve010/)

### Inferences
- A pro would put these in the model before screens: booking-model choice per department (a clinic may need all three), 初診/再診 as appointment types with different durations, per-menu slot rules, emergency/walk-in insertion and "shift the rest" operations, stop/limit controls for sudden closure, phone-booking entry as a first-class staff flow, and two-way EMR integration scope.
- Patient and staff flows are separate products in practice: patient entry points split by 初診/再診/same-day; staff side needs 受付 status tracking (the status names were not found in any source; do not invent a canonical list).

### Gaps
- Not retrieved: official help centers/manuals for Airリザーブ, CLINICS (Medley), メディカルフォース, 予約ラボ, ドクターキューブ, Apotool&Box, デンタルアイ (search returned nothing; US-only search tool). No screenshots or screen-level flows. No dental-specific source. No independent (non-vendor) case study. No data on waitlist (キャンセル待ち) behavior beyond queue products.
- 診察券アプリ details only via EPARK page. マイナ保険証 at reception was not researched in this round (no source retrieved).

## 2. Regulation: MHLW guideline, PPC personal information, 3省2ガイドライン (exact section names)

### Takeaway
Current MHLW guideline is 第7.0版 (令和8年6月; 5 編: 概説, 経営管理, 企画管理, システム運用, 保守委託機関). For a SaaS, the clinic remains accountable and must agree 責任分界 with the vendor; the vendor-side guideline is the 総務省・経産省 2省ガイドライン 第2.0版 (令和7年3月28日). Product-relevant hard requirements: user identification/authentication, least-privilege access rights, ID inventory, access-log collection/review, 二要素認証 (from 令和9年度 for new/replaced systems), password rules. 病歴/診療情報 are 要配慮個人情報.

### Cited Findings
- 第7.0版 composition and files (概説編 令和8年6月, 経営管理編 6月, 企画管理編 5月, システム運用編 6月, 保守委託機関編); 7.0 adds 保守委託機関編 for small providers who outsource all server updates (compliance with it is deemed to cover other 編). — [MHLW page listing](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/stf/shingi/0000516275_00006.html), [MHLW WG slides 2026-05-29](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/content/10808000/001102596.pdf)
- Revision history: 6.0 = 令和5年5月, 7.0 = 令和8年6月; 7.0 changes include 二要素認証 target clarified (client apps and servers), password rule changes (reuse banned, account lock added, periodic change removed), cloud-native EMR promotion, supply chain risk, 医療機関と事業者との役割分担 and risk communication. — [MHLW WG slides](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/content/10808000/001102596.pdf)
- システム運用編 section "１４．認証・認可に関する安全管理措置" (遵守事項): ① identify/authenticate users and document the procedure; ② keep ID/password known only to the user; ④ grant access rights on application by the user's department with confirmation by the applicant department's manager; ⑤ for systems expected to be running at 令和9年4月1日, when newly introduced or replaced, adopt 二要素認証 (client app login; server OS), with transitional relief until the next system renewal; ⑥ password: block guessable passwords, no reuse across systems, store hashed, identity check logged when an operator resets, lock out after repeated failures; ⑧ manage IDs in a ledger and inventory periodically, delete unneeded. — [MHLW 7.0 システム運用編](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/content/10808000/001716295.pdf)
- 14.1.1: applies "even for small institutions with few users"; password length 8+ chars with mixed alphanumerics when 二要素認証 used (PIN 4+), 13+ chars otherwise; periodic change not required. — [MHLW 7.0 システム運用編](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/content/10808000/001716295.pdf)
- 14.2 アクセス権限の管理: classify information, define permissions per information class and per user/user group, minimum necessary; finer-grained permissions (view/update/execute/add) reduce risk; review permissions upon role changes; for cloud services, vendor defaults/policy updates can silently change access rights, so operators must detect unintended setting changes (preferably automatically). — [MHLW 7.0 システム運用編](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/content/10808000/001716295.pdf)
- 17.1 証跡のレビュー: collect access logs for all resources containing personal information and review regularly (threshold/screening based, not every line by eye); protect logs from access/deletion/tampering/addition; synchronize time; alerts for emergencies; agree log handling with outsourcing vendor; if the system has no access-log function, keep an operation journal (operator and operation) as substitute. 17.2 監査の実施の支援: provide evidence for audits. — [MHLW 7.0 システム運用編](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/content/10808000/001716295.pdf)
- 7.2.2 患者等に診療情報等を提供する場合の外部からのアクセス: patient-facing access is generally read-only and patients need simpler mechanisms than staff; apply network measures as for staff remote access. — [MHLW 7.0 システム運用編](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/content/10808000/001716295.pdf)
- 企画管理編 table of contents (main headings): 管理体系; 責任分界; 安全管理のための体制と責任・権限; 規程・文書類の整備; エビデンス; リスクマネジメント; 人的管理 (職員管理, 事業者管理, 教育・訓練, 事業者選定・契約); 情報管理; 資産管理; 点検・監査; 非常時対応とBCP; サイバーセキュリティ; "１３．医療情報システムの利用者に関する認証等及び権限"; 電子署名; 技術的な安全管理対策の管理; 紙媒体の電子化. On SaaS: the application layer's management/responsibility is entrusted to the vendor, so 責任分界 must be agreed with the vendor and concrete measures decided with them. — [MHLW 7.0 企画管理編](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/content/10808000/001716292.pdf)
- 個人情報ガイダンス (PPC+MHLW, 令和8年4月一部改正) "Ⅱ 用語の定義等 ３．要配慮個人情報（法第２条第３項）": 病歴, 診療・調剤情報, 健診結果, 障害 etc. are 要配慮個人情報; acquisition and third-party provision need consent in principle; opt-out third-party provision is not allowed; 委託 is an exception. — [PPC/MHLW guidance PDF](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/content/001470633.pdf)
- Same guidance "Ⅳ ７．安全管理措置、従業者の監督及び委託先の監督（法第２３条～第２５条）": technical measures include access control (ID/password auth; access limited to what each staff member's duties need; two-factor "desirable"), keeping access records, periodic check for abnormal records, firewall, monitoring external access, vulnerability patches. 委託先の監督 means contract terms on safeguards, periodic checks, and re-delegation controls; the clinic can be held responsible for a sub-contractor's improper handling. — [PPC/MHLW guidance PDF](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/content/001470633.pdf)
- 2省ガイドライン 第2.0版: published 令和7年3月28日 by 総務省 and 経産省 (rev. of 1.1); adds FAQ, a service-specification compliance disclosure reference and a sample SLA; PDF at https://www.meti.go.jp/policy/mono_info_service/healthcare/01gl_20250328.pdf (not downloaded). — [総務省 press release](https://www.soumu.go.jp/menu_news/s-news/01ryutsu06_02000427.html)

### Inferences
- Product-level implications (mine): per-user accounts for every staff member (no shared reception login), role-scoped permissions with minimum necessary, access log of who viewed/changed patient data, ID inventory screen (list/disable users), lockout and 2FA-capable login, safe password reset flow with identity verification, patient-side read-limited and simple, notification text without diagnosis (diagnosis would be 要配慮個人情報 leaving the system via LINE/SMS). A booking-only SaaS may or may not hold 診療情報; whether 問診 content (symptoms) makes it 医療情報システム scope should be decided by the vendor/clinic, I did not find a source that settles this for booking-only tools.
- A pro designing this must surface 責任分界 and 委託 supervision as product concerns (data processing terms, log provision, export on termination), not only legal footnotes.

### Gaps
- 7.0 経営管理編 and 保守委託機関編 not read in detail; 概説編 only skimmed. 2省ガイドライン 2.0 PDF not downloaded, so its chapter names are not cited. Whether booking-only SaaS falls under "医療情報システム" not confirmed. 二要素認証 applies to 医療情報を扱う systems; whether patient-facing booking needs it was not found (patient side described only as simple, read-only). Dates conflict across secondary sources for publication (6/29 notice vs 7/15 per one explainer); I rely on the MHLW file labels (令和8年6月).

## 3. B2B SaaS structure: roles/permissions, audit logs, bulk actions, onboarding

### Takeaway
Retrieved sources support specific patterns (least privilege; separate role definitions from assignments; audit logs for permission changes; bulk-action bar with undo/confirmation; empty states as onboarding). Several requested sources (SmartHR design system, GitLab Pajamas, Polaris admin, EightShapes, Cooper) were not retrieved; the checklist below mixes cited items and flagged inference.

### Cited Findings
- Bulk actions (NN/g): Select All, a contextual action bar, clear feedback and undo. — [NN/g video](https://www.nngroup.com/videos/bulk-actions-design-guidelines/)
- Carbon data table: tri-state header checkbox; batch action bar appears on selection, exit via cancel/deselect; per-row actions disabled in batch mode; confirmation for deletes especially if irreversible or multiple items. — [Carbon data table](https://carbondesignsystem.com/components/data-table/usage/), [Carbon common actions](https://carbondesignsystem.com/patterns/common-actions/)
- NN/g: undo should exist but prevention is better; separate consequential actions from benign ones spatially. — [NN/g proximity](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/proximity-consequential-options/)
- Polaris index table: verb+noun labels for bulk actions; hide only on narrow screens when not essential. — [Polaris index table](https://polaris-react.shopify.com/components/tables/index-table)
- Data tables' four user tasks (NN/g; title only retrieved). — [NN/g data tables](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/data-tables/)
- RBAC (Microsoft docs, implementation guidance not UX): least privilege by default; small stable catalog of role definitions with variation in assignment layer (community answer, weak); clear role boundaries; audit logs record configuration/permission changes separately from sign-in logs; separate administration from audit so an admin cannot erase own actions; narrow roles for audit. — [Intune RBAC](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/intune/fundamentals/role-based-access-control/overview), [Entra audit/roles](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/entra/identity/role-based-access-control/custom-overview), [Purview audit activities](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/purview/audit-log-activities), [PAM roles](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-identity-manager/pam/defining-roles-for-pam)
- Empty states / first run: NN/g says empty states are common in unconfigured complex apps, say what the area will show and give a direct link to the first task; in-context cues beat forced tours. Carbon: empty state as launch point for optional onboarding; starter content cuts configuration time; "configuration required" and permission-denied are error states needing a next step. Atlassian: short CTAs with imperative verbs, 1-2 words. Polaris: onboarding tasks as checklist whose illustrations change when complete. — [NN/g empty states](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/empty-state-interface-design/), [Carbon empty states](https://carbondesignsystem.com/patterns/empty-states-pattern/), [Atlassian empty state](https://atlassian.design/foundations/content/designing-messages/empty-state), [Polaris illustrations](https://polaris-react.shopify.com/design/illustrations)
- (Carried from round 1) NN/g complex-app strategy: multiple roles, workflows across people, domain experts. — [NN/g](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/strategies-complex-application-design/)
- Clinic-specific: the MHLW 7.0 rules in Section 2 (access-right request/approval, ID inventory, access log review, cloud default-setting drift) are the primary-source basis for permissions/audit features in a clinic SaaS.

### Inferences (checklist a pro would not miss for this case; judge outputs against it)
1. Roles: 院長/管理者, 受付, 医師, 看護師 (and possibly 事務長, vendor support, read-only/auditor) with a role x object x action matrix (view/create/edit/cancel/delete/export; patient detail incl. 問診; settings; user management; logs). Separate role definition from assignment; scope per location/department/practitioner. Who can see 問診 content and patient contact data is a decision, not a default.
2. User lifecycle: invite, role assignment with approval trail, role change, offboarding same day, last-admin protection (cannot remove the last 管理者), password reset by admin with identity check, 2FA enrollment, session timeout on shared reception PCs.
3. Audit log: who/what/when/which record for view (patient detail), create/change/cancel appointments, permission and settings changes, export, login/failure; read-only, tamper-resistant, filterable, exportable; retention period stated; who may view logs; vendor access (support impersonation) must be logged and disclosed.
4. Bulk operations: clinic-wide closure/holiday (cancel and notify N affected appointments with a preview of who is affected and the message), shift template apply/copy week, slot open/close ranges, bulk reminder resend, CSV import of existing patients/appointments, CSV export of patients/appointments/logs. Needs preview, count, confirm, progress, partial-failure report and undo/rollback per the sources above.
5. Org onboarding / first run: create clinic, departments, rooms/equipment, practitioners and weekly shifts, appointment types (初診/再診/menus, durations), booking model per department, business hours/holidays, notification channel setup (LINE/SMS/email) and templates, cancellation policy, 問診 form, invite staff, publish patient booking URL; checklist with empty states and a "test booking" preview; data migration from the prior system or paper.
6. Settings architecture: clinic-level vs department/practitioner-level vs user-level; defaults and overrides visible; settings changes logged and previewed for effect on existing bookings (changing slot length with future bookings).
7. Multi-location: location switcher, per-location roles, consolidated view for 院長, cross-location patient identity.
8. Notifications: staff-side (new booking, cancellation, queue changes) and patient-side templates editable with preview; no sensitive content in message body; opt-out; delivery failure visibility.
9. Data lifecycle: export on termination, retention/deletion, backup/ disaster mode (what staff do when the system is down: paper fallback list).

### Gaps
- Not retrieved: SmartHR design system/blog, GitLab Pajamas, Polaris admin settings patterns, Atlassian permission UX, EightShapes, Cooper "complex applications", NN/g articles on permissions/audit/import. RBAC sources above are Microsoft implementation docs, not UX guidance. No source found on multi-tenant first-run for clinics specifically, on undo timing, or on selection persistence across pagination. Items in the Inferences list are my synthesis.

## 4. Domain rules and healthcare UX specifics (elderly, accessibility, trust)

### Takeaway
Retrieved evidence supports: keep phone/window channels, large type and fewer steps, tolerant input (NN/g seniors), and privacy-conscious messaging. JIS X 8341-3, マイナ保険証 reception flow, children/proxy rules and 無断キャンセル fee rules were not researched with primary sources.

### Cited Findings
- Seniors (NN/g, from round 1): small type/targets are repeated barriers; rigid input formats frustrate; accept multiple formats, clear error messages. — [NN/g](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/usability-for-senior-citizens/)
- Vendor advice: keep phone with role split, proxy booking, window next-visit booking, large text, fewer steps, plain language, LINE notices as stepping stone. — [MEDISMA](https://medisma.jp/column/reserve010/)
- Pediatric need: sibling simultaneous booking. (vendor) — [m3 digikar](https://digikar.m3.com/articles/system/article69)
- Patient-facing access should be simple and generally read-only. — [MHLW 7.0 システム運用編 7.2.2](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/content/10808000/001716295.pdf)
- Two-way staff concern: guideline 17.1 alternative of an operation journal when logs are absent implies manual fallback must be designed. — [MHLW 7.0 システム運用編](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/content/10808000/001716295.pdf)

### Inferences
- A pro would also specify: proxy booking (parent/guardian/caregiver booking for a person; who consents and receives reminders), phone bookings entered by reception with the same rules, 初診 identity matching (名寄せ, duplicate patient records) and 診察券番号 as key, late arrival/no-show handling as staff-defined states, a no-show policy shown before booking (text on patient screens), no diagnosis in notifications, privacy statement at the point of entering 問診 (purpose of use, 要配慮個人情報 consent), 24h booking vs staff-hours handling of urgent symptoms (a "call us if urgent/emergency" notice).

### Gaps
- Not researched: JIS X 8341-3 criteria (level targets), マイナ保険証/オンライン資格確認 reception flow, legal basis for cancellation fees, 医師法 constraints on online first visits, waitlist (キャンセル待ち) evidence, HIPAA-style patient trust studies. No NN/g source on privacy messaging retrieved.
