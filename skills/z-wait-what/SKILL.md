Wait — I have lost track of where this is going. Say that explanation again. Put the background you skipped first, unpack any term you left unopened, and use the wording from `CONTEXT.md` (the one `CONTEXT-MAP.md` points at, if there are several). Write it in plain Japanese following the `z-japanese-proofreading` norms if you are writing in Japanese, or in ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English if you are writing in English.
